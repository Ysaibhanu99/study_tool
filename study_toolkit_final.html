Why students procrastinate — and what works
2-minute start rule
Tell yourself you'll work for only 2 minutes. Starting is the hardest part — once you're in, momentum takes over.
Shrink the task
"Write essay" is paralyzing. "Write one opening sentence" is not. Break it down until the next step is laughably small.
Phone in another room
Seeing your phone reduces cognitive capacity. Physical distance works better than willpower every time.
Reward yourself intentionally
Plan the reward before starting. "After this session I'll watch one episode." Guilt-free, not reactive scrolling.
Body doubling
Study alongside someone — in person or virtually. Presence of others activates accountability without speaking.
Know your trigger
Boredom, anxiety, perfectionism, resentment — each needs a different fix. Identify yours first.
